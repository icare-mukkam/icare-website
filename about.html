🏢 Our Story

iCare Mukkam was established in June 2017 with a simple mission — to provide honest, quality mobile repair services to the people of Mukkam and nearby areas.

Over the years, we grew from a small service center to a complete tech solutions provider under our online brand fixG. We now offer mobile sales, laptop service, accessories, spare parts and advanced motherboard-level repairs.

Our commitment to quality and customer satisfaction has earned us 35+ Google reviews and thousands of happy customers across Kozhikode district.
